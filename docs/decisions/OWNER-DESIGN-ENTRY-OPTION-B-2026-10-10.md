# Owner decision — Option B: finish pre-design research

## Exact owner instruction

> I choose Vellistry design-entry Option B: complete outstanding pre-design research and owner-critical decisions before prototypes.

**Decision date:** 2026-10-10. The quoted instruction is the authority; no unavailable clock time, chat URL, separate implementation approval or third-party consent is invented.

## Meaning and authorization

- **Option B selected.** Complete source-grounded pre-design research, unresolved author/competitor discovery, accessibility and editing investigation, privacy/security requirements, format fidelity alternatives and necessary owner-critical choices **before** generating Figma Make/Lovable prototypes or other interactive interface mockups.
- **Research is permitted**, including reading first-party/official material, running synthetic/local disposable experiments, preparing participant scripts, collecting *consented* real-author interviews and accessibility observations, documenting alternative approaches and bringing owner decisions for explicit approval.
- **No UI/UX prototype authorization** is given: no Figma Make, Lovable, generated screens, canonical Figma Design, click-through prototypes, or user testing that depends on an unapproved new interface. Concept requirements and non-interactive research material are allowed.
- **No authorization** is given for production feature implementation, migration/deployment, paid APIs/services, confidential manuscript uploads to external tools or a public launch.
- Existing Next.js/NestJS/Supabase scaffold and completed six-pair *research experiments* remain preserved. This decision does not undo merges or mean those experiments fully satisfied research acceptance.

## Preconditions for submitting a future design-entry request

The owner must receive a source-traced, prioritized closeout packet that distinguishes **PASS**, **FAIL**, **BLOCKED**, **NOT RUN**, and **owner decision needed**, with particular attention to:

1. **R02/R03 and R05/R16**: real author jobs-to-be-done/interviews without prototype anchoring, independent competitor walkthrough evidence, keyboard/IME/accessibility task risk, and honest observed results. No fabricated participant outcomes.
2. **R01/R06**: full-body compact/expanded guidance review, a versioned operational proposal (including distinct-meaning variants) and **explicit owner approval** for active/default rule identities; 260 immutable originals retained and no automatic activation.
3. **R15/K2B/R18**: threat and dataflow model for manuscript, user switching, SSR/session/logout, local drafts, Storage/exports, backup and deletion; validated integration risk map, without mislabeling separate disposable database proofs as production security.
4. **R11/R07**: decide and validate a credible free document round-trip path addressing Calibre's measured Heading-style loss, portable data/restore expectations, story-time/canon review and model-extraction uncertainty.
5. **R08/R19/R21**: owner-reviewed optional provider terms, actual legally usable free/local model feasibility, consent and BYOK flow, quality evaluation of real outputs (where lawful/feasible) with human scoring; otherwise explicitly narrow the optional AI promise and seek owner approval.
6. **R14/R21/R18**: licensing/distribution, Ghana/international privacy applicability, manuscript ownership and retention, zero-mandatory-paid operations, encrypted offsite backup responsibilities and RPO/RTO options with actual evidence or qualified expert review.

Treat the distinction between **research-before-design decisions** and **mandatory before real implementation/public release** as explicit. Strict Option B means owner-critical unknowns and usable research evidence precede prototypes; it does not require shipping a secure product before drawing UI, nor authorize skipping any launch-critical requirements.
