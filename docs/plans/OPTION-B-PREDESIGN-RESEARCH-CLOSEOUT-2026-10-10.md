## Wave 1: two currently active research tracks

### A — R02/R03 writer and comparator research

Use the existing 11 journey tasks from [MVP author journeys](../requirements/MVP-USER-JOURNEYS-AND-ACCEPTANCE-2026-10-09.md) and blank [Pair 02 observation sheet](../research/PAIR-02-AUTHOR-OBSERVATIONS-TEMPLATE.csv). Do not claim completed interviews.

1. Recruit writers from different workflows (manual novelist, serial writer, complex world-builder, experienced document-platform author). Document *actual* eligibility and consent, or mark recruitment **BLOCKED**.
2. Interview current unassisted processes **without a new prototype**. Authors may demonstrate tools they already use or explain last completed work, including revisions, canon notes, losing edits, model trust, export, rule scope and AI-OFF. Do not ask them to upload personal manuscripts.
3. Observe representative tasks when feasible using *participant-owned existing tools* and/or purely verbal story scenarios. Clearly mark concept reactions separately from observed actions and never infer that an unbuilt interface was tested.
4. Compare competitor claims with real accessible free/no-paid walkthrough evidence where lawful; distinguish vendor documentation, hands-on observation and forum anecdotes.
5. Produce observations tied to journey IDs, task result, error, device/accessibility context if volunteered, verbatim consented quote, limitations and prioritized design requirements.

**Exit gate:** a real, documented user-evidence packet or explicit owner review of recruitment/design-study blockage. Empty observation templates or simulated interview transcripts **cannot** meet this gate.

### B — R01 active source mapping decisions

Start with the 260 original immutable source records and [R01 owner proposal](../research/R01-134-CONTROL-OWNER-MAPPING-PROPOSAL-2026-10-09.md). Do not modify or collapse original compact/expanded source hashes.

1. Compare **full text** of all 130 compact/expanded pairs, not only casefold titles; mark equivalent, partial-overlap, genuinely distinct or unresolved with source IDs and review evidence. In particular AR-02/04/06/09 retain separate candidate meanings until owner decision.
2. Prepare per-candidate versioned operational ID, exact source fingerprint/variant, intent label, proposed default on/off, target book/chapter scope, possible conflict, exception override and reason. Preserve 134 as a **proposal**, not a frozen count or compulsory active UI controls.
3. Report risks of overconstraint, disabled-rule leakage and effective provider request collision. Keep custom/author-created guidance separate from immutable sources.
4. Ask owner to **approve/revise/reject the specific active mapping and defaults**. Do not activate the rules, deploy code, or infer approval from Option B.

**Exit gate:** owner decision evidenced at per-rule source mapping/default level, or an explicit **BLOCKED ON OWNER** status with the review packet; no silent approval.

## Evidence and closeout rules across all waves

Each research record must identify source(s), question, hypothesis, alternative, reproducible test or interview protocol, actual observations, limitations, cost/license/privacy risk, confidence, status **PASS / FAIL / BLOCKED / NOT RUN / OWNER DECISION NEEDED**, and decision owner. The [G01–G10 readiness questions](../research/MVP-READINESS-GATES-AND-ARCHITECTURE-DECISION-QUESTIONS-2026-10-09.md) remain tracked, but older reports are historical, not a basis to erase newly discovered gaps.

**Pre-design owner-critical decisions** include target author/AI-OFF task priorities, authentic rule mapping, planned private manuscript boundaries, fidelity/loss policy, Story Profiles proposal authority, optional model/privacy/fallback path, ownership/retention/legal operating assumptions, and the feasibility constraints the UI must transparently communicate. Real production implementation and release security certifications are **later gates** and cannot be performed merely by generating a prototype; research should still specify them before design.

**Stop condition:** No UI exploration, screen generation, prototype user testing, Figma write, production feature coding, paid inference or real confidential manuscript transfer until owner reviews the resulting closeout dossier and explicitly changes the design-entry decision. Only the owner can decide that remaining unavoidable unknowns are appropriately scoped for a subsequent stage; unanswered critical pre-design decisions cannot be reclassified as PASS.
