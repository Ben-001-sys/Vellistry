# R05 Research ExecutionBundle — editor and document-production feasibility

## Objective
Examine mature rich-text/manuscript editing candidates, actual commercial-license boundaries and whether professional book publishing features are supplied as part of the editor or require separate work.

## Work performed
- Reviewed upstream framework/project licenses and official vendor documentation for ProseMirror, Tiptap, Lexical, Slate, Plate, CKEditor 5 and TinyMCE 8.
- Distinguished general document editing from DOCX import/export, EPUB production and print-ready PDF layout.
- Described 12 comparative author-oriented trials, licensing questions, and product claims requiring hands-on verification.
- Made no package selection, purchase, source-tree commitment or prototype.

## FACT — documentary support
- Multiple core editor frameworks are MIT-licensed but shipping a whole authoring product involves additional features and dependencies.
- Tiptap Pages and Conversion are separately gated Pro offerings in current official documentation.
- CKEditor 5 is GPL 2+/commercial with separately licensed premium features and cloud-conversion components.
- TinyMCE 8 documents specific self-hosted commercial license/key-manager requirements.
- Reedsy markets EPUB and print-PDF book formatting; a conventional rich-text engine's capabilities must not be equated to print production merely on that basis.

## INTERPRETATION
- Treating 'editor and publishing pipeline' as one checkbox would obscure substantial licensing and engineering effort.
- MIT-license status makes candidates *eligible for investigation*, not automatically preferred or qualified.
- Real import/export fidelity and author ergonomics may dominate editorial satisfaction more than the presence of a feature button.

## PROPOSAL — NOT APPROVED
- Conduct a bounded equivalent-fixture trial of two or three representative editor approaches.
- Use consistent long-form manuscript and Word fixtures and score observed fidelity, stability, keyboard/screen-reader support, AI proposal range safety and cost.
- Audit each installed package and paid integration before committing a production deployment model.

## Verification and limitations
- **No hands-on editor build or performance benchmark executed.**
- No commercial license cleared, legal conclusion given, paid plan purchased, vendor deployment selected, EPUBCheck validation performed or print-PDF compliance established.
- Findings are grounded in current official published documents only.
- Application architecture, provider/MCP architecture, editor choice and MVP feature gates all remain open.
